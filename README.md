## Amazon ML School — Entity Resolution Challenge

### Overview
Participated in the Amazon ML School challenge focused on matching business entities across three data sources. Achieved a **score of 84.876523%** by working on the entity-matching problem involving inconsistent business names and addresses.

### Approach & Methodology
- **Data Preprocessing:** Processed business records and handled inconsistencies in names and addresses.
- **Text Normalization:** Addressed variations in abbreviations, punctuation, spelling, legal suffixes, and address formats.
- **Candidate Generation:** Narrowed down potential matches using a blocking strategy to reduce unnecessary record comparisons.
- **Entity Matching:** Compared candidate business records to identify matching entities across multiple sources.
- **Evaluation:** Focused on precision-oriented matching and validation using the challenge's evaluation metric.
- **Submission Generation:** Prepared the required `matching_results.tsv` and `candidate_pairs.tsv` output files.

### Results

- **Challenge Domain:** Entity Resolution & Record Linkage
- **Key Focus:** Accurate business record matching across multiple data sources.
